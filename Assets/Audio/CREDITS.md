# Explorer Dash – Audio Credits

All music and sound effects in `Assets/Audio` are **original and procedurally generated**
by `tools/audio/generate_audio.py` (Python + numpy/scipy, encoded to OGG Vorbis with ffmpeg/libvorbis).
No samples, recordings, or third-party/CC0 assets are used.

Music is an original composition: "Canopy Scramble" (D Dorian, 6/8) plus title and game-over stings.
It is intentionally unrelated to any existing film or game theme.
